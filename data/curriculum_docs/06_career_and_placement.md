# Career Services: Placement Policy and Salary Guidance

## Readiness statuses
Learners are marked Ready for Placement, In Progress, Needs Review or Dropped. A learner becomes Ready for Placement after completing at least 12 modules and achieving a Python assignment score of at least 80. Needs Review means the learner is below the milestone for their week and should meet an advisor.

## Hiring partners
Sprints works with Egyptian employers including Fawry, Swvl, Vodafone Egypt and e& Egypt. Fawry is a tier-1 partner. Every placement recommendation for a tier-1 partner must be approved by a human career expert before it is shown to the learner or shared with the employer.

## Salary expectations
Target salaries are recorded in Egyptian pounds (EGP) per month. Advisors compare the target salary with the learner's track and score. Targets far above the typical range for the track should be discussed during coaching. Final offers always come from the employer.

## Escalation
Placement approvals, disputes about grades and learners at risk of dropping out are escalated to a human expert. The automated system may recommend but never finalizes a tier-1 placement.
